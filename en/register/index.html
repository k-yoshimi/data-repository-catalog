Register a dataset

Apply to register a dataset in the ISSP Data Repository. Enter the DOI of the paper and the title, authors, ORCID iDs and reference are looked up and filled into the application form for you. Without a DOI, you can apply from a blank form.

Start from the paper's DOI

Enter the DOI of the paper (or dataset) that the data belongs to.

The DOI is looked up at doi.org and OpenAlex; only the DOI you enter is sent.

Apply without a DOI

If the paper is not published yet, or the data is a database or software without a paper, fill in every field from a blank form.

Open a blank application form ↗ The Google Form opens in a new tab.
