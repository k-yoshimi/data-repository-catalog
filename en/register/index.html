Register a dataset

Apply to register a dataset in the ISSP Data Repository. Enter the DOI of the paper and the title, authors, ORCID iDs and reference are looked up and filled into the application form for you. Without a DOI, you can apply from a blank form.

How registration works

  1. Fill in the application form from this page and submit it.
  2. The data repository team reviews the application.
  3. Once accepted, a page (repository) for publishing your data is created on the ISSP GitLab.
  4. Its URL and the information for adding and editing the data are sent to the Contact e-mail address.

Before you apply

  • Size: if your repository needs more than 10 GB, write the size you need in the form.
  • License: an open license such as CC BY 4.0 is recommended.
  • Title: letters, digits, emojis, "_", ".", "-" and spaces; it must start with a letter or digit.
  • Format: About, References and Related software accept Markdown.
  • Contact: the e-mail address given as Contact is shown on the public page.

Questions: datarepo__at__issp.u-tokyo.ac.jp (replace __at__ with @)

Start from the paper's DOI

Enter the DOI of the paper (or dataset) that the data belongs to.

The DOI is looked up at doi.org and OpenAlex; only the DOI you enter is sent.

Apply without a DOI

If the paper is not published yet, or the data is a database or software without a paper, fill in every field from a blank form.
