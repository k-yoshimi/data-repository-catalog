概要

Density-functional-theory calculations revealed that the formation of tilted Si–C bonds between scanning Si probes and diamond (001) surfaces, coupled with the reordering of surface C–C dimers, are critical factors for achieving atomic resolution. The mechanism of high-resolution AFM imaging was elucidated by force spectroscopy combined with first-principles calculations. This database provides the input files and the results of the DFT calculations by OpenMX.

Atomic Observation on Diamond (001) Surfaces with Near-Contact Atomic Force Microscopy

About

Density-functional-theory calculations revealed that the formation of tilted Si-C bonds between scanning Si probes and diamond (001) surfaces, coupled with the reordering of surface C-C dimers, are critical factors for achieving atomic resolution. The mechanism of high-resolution AFM imaging was elucidated by force spectroscopy combined with first-principles calculations. This database provides the input files and the results of the DFT calculations by OpenMX.

Usage

  • Install Database

    • Using git

      git clone https://isspns-gitlab.issp.u-tokyo.ac.jp/masahiro.fukuda/C001_dimer_chain_AFM.git

    • On browser Click the download button of this project.

  • Data info

    • This database includes input files and results of AFM simulation by DFT.
      • data/calc/C001
        • Carbon dimer chains on the C(001) diamond surface.
      • data/calc/HC001
        • Hydrogenated carbon dimer chains on the C(001) diamond surface.
      • data/calc/C001_vacancy_for_FigS1
        • Carbon dimer chains on the C(001) diamond surface with vacancy shown in Fig.S1.
      • data/calc/Si001_vacancy_for_FigS1
        • Carbon dimer chains on the Si(001) diamond surface with vacancy shown in Fig.S1.
      • data/Data_of_Fig3
        • Raw data for ploting Fig.3 in the paper.
      • data/Data_of_Fig4
        • Raw data for ploting Fig.4 in the paper.
